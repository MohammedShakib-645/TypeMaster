🖐️ 1. Home Row Foundation Rules

The Home Row is your anchor position on the keyboard. After striking any upper or lower key, your fingers must automatically return to these positions:

  • Left Hand: Pinky on A, Ring on S, Middle on D, Index on F
  • Right Hand: Index on J, Middle on K, Ring on L, Pinky on ;
  • Thumbs: Rest gently on Spacebar

🚀 4. Golden Rules for 100+ WPM Typing Speed

  • Never Look at the Keyboard: Build motor memory by keeping your eyes on the screen at all times.
  • Prioritize Accuracy Over Speed: Speed naturally accelerates once 98%+ accuracy is mastered.
  • Maintain Rhythmic Cadence: Type with a steady beat rather than irregular fast bursts.
  • Use Opposite Shift Key: When capitalizing a left-hand character, press Shift with your right pinky.
