🪑 Proper Typing Posture
🪑
Sit Up Straight
Back straight and supported. Avoid slouching. Your spine should be aligned and comfortable throughout your session.
🦵
Feet Flat
Both feet flat on the floor. Knees at a 90° angle. This prevents lower back strain during extended typing sessions.
💪
Elbows at 90°
Elbows bent at ~90 degrees. Upper arms close to body. Shoulders relaxed and not raised up toward your ears.
✋
Level Wrists
Wrists level with the keyboard — not bent up or down. Avoid resting wrists on the desk while actively typing keys.
👁️
Eyes on Screen
Look at the screen, NOT the keyboard! Screen at eye level, arm's length away. Never look at your hands while typing.
⏰
Take Breaks
Every 30 minutes, rest for 5 minutes. Shake wrists, stand up, and look away from screen to prevent repetitive strain injuries.

🖐️ 1. Home Row Anchor Position

The Home Row (A S D F — J K L ;) is your starting and return position. Always return your fingers here after striking any key.

👈 Left Hand Placement:
• Pinky: A · Ring: S · Middle: D · Index: F
👉 Right Hand Placement:
• Index: J · Middle: K · Ring: L · Pinky: ;
👍 Thumbs:
• Rest lightly on Spacebar

🧘 2. Posture & Ergonomics

  • Sit up straight with your back fully supported by your chair.
  • Keep elbows bent at approximately 90 degrees close to your torso.
  • Keep wrists level and floating: Avoid resting wrists on desk while typing.
  • Position computer screen at eye level, about 45–70 cm away.
  • Rest for 5 minutes every 30 minutes to prevent eye and wrist strain.

🚀 5. Golden Rules for Rapid 100+ WPM Typing

1. Never Look at the Keyboard
Trust your finger muscle memory. Looking down slows typing by 70%.
2. Accuracy First, Speed Second
Aim for 98%+ accuracy. Speed naturally accelerates once accuracy is solid.
3. Maintain a Steady Rhythm
Type with consistent keypress intervals rather than erratic fast-slow bursts.
4. Use Opposite Hand for Shift
If key is on left hand (e.g. A), press Shift with your right pinky.
5. Always Return to Home Row
After striking upper or lower keys, immediately return fingers to ASDF JKL;.
6. Daily 15-Minute Focused Practice
Consistent daily practice yields 3x faster progress than long sporadic sessions.

TypeMaster Web Application

Install TypeMaster directly on your Android, iPhone, Windows, or Mac for full-screen mode, 100% offline practice, and maximum performance!

📱 Android & Chrome Installation
  1. Tap the ⋮ (three dots menu) at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Select "Install App" or "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Click Install to add TypeMaster to your app drawer.
🍏 iPhone & Safari Installation
  1. Open TypeMaster in Safari on your iPhone/iPad.
  2. Tap the Share button (⎋) at the bottom of Safari.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen (➕)".
💻 Windows & Mac Desktop App
  1. Click the Install icon (⊕) in your browser's address bar.
  2. Or click the Install App button above to launch as a standalone desktop app.
